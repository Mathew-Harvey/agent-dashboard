## CV Selection Strategy

### Use Digital PM CV when:
- Role is: Digital Project Manager, Product Owner, Delivery Manager, Scrum Master
- Sector is: Healthcare, Government, Tech, SaaS, Digital Agency
- Keywords mention: Agile, Transformation, Stakeholder Engagement, WCAG, Accessibility
- Company needs: Strategic planning, governance, change management

**Strengths:**
- Emphasizes transformation & governance
- Healthcare sector credibility (WAPHA-targeted)
- Accessibility champion positioning
- Product Owner experience highlighted

### Use Event Manager CV when:
- Role is: Event Manager, Event Coordinator, Experience Manager, Community Manager
- Sector is: Sports, Entertainment, NFP, Community, Tourism
- Keywords mention: Logistics, On-site, Hybrid events, Fan experience, Ticketing
- Company needs: Delivery focus, team coordination, vendor management

**Strengths:**
- Olympic/Paralympic credibility
- Live event logistics expertise
- Hybrid/digital event pivot experience
- Sports & community sector track record

## Cover Letter Framework (Skye's Voice)

### Opening (2 sentences)
**Template:**
> I'm writing to express my interest in the [ROLE] role at [COMPANY]. With [X] years of experience [RELEVANT ACHIEVEMENT], I'm [EXCITED/DRAWN TO/EAGER] to [SPECIFIC VALUE PROP ALIGNED WITH JOB].

**Skye's style notes:**
- Warm but professional
- Direct and confident (no "I believe I would be" — state facts)
- Lead with years of experience or standout achievement

### Middle (2-3 paragraphs)

**Paragraph 1: Relevant experience bridge**
- Connect most relevant role to job requirements
- Mention 2-3 specific deliverables/outcomes
- Example: "In my role as [X], I [ACTION] resulting in [OUTCOME]"

**Paragraph 2: Unique value / differentiator**
- What makes Skye stand out for THIS role?
- Digital + Events hybrid if relevant
- Transformation/change management angle if applicable
- Community/stakeholder engagement strength

**Paragraph 3: Cultural/practical fit**
- Remote readiness (if WFH role)
- WA timezone capability
- Team collaboration style
- Passion for the sector/mission

### Closing
**Template:**
> Thank you for considering my application. I've attached my CV and would be glad to provide more details or connect for a conversation.

> Warm regards,  
> Skye Harvey

**Style notes:**
- "Warm regards" is her signature
- Keep it short
- No desperate language
- Confidence without arrogance

## Tailoring Rules for Application Packages

### When generating cover letters:
1. **Open the job description** — identify top 3-5 requirements
2. **Select relevant CV** — Digital PM or Event Manager based on role type
3. **Extract Skye's matching experience** from CV
4. **Draft cover letter** matching the framework above
5. **Align language** — use job posting keywords naturally in cover letter
6. **Keep it 1 page max** — 3-4 paragraphs, ~300-400 words

### When tailoring CVs:
- **Usually no changes needed** — her CVs are already well-structured
- **Exception:** If job description has specific technical requirements not listed, add them to "Tools & Platforms" if Skye has them
- **Don't invent skills** — only include what she actually has

### Selection Criteria (government/NFP roles):
If job includes selection criteria questions:

**Format:**
```
Criterion: [EXACT CRITERION TEXT]

[2-3 paragraph response with specific examples]

Example: 
- When I [ACTION] at [ORGANIZATION]
- This resulted in [OUTCOME]
- Demonstrating [SKILL/COMPETENCY FROM CRITERION]
```

**Approach:**
- Use STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result)
- Pull specific examples from her work history
- Quantify outcomes where possible
- Reference relevant education/certifications

## Cover Letter Tone & Language Guidelines

### ✅ Do:
- Be direct and confident
- Use specific examples
- Show genuine interest in the role/company
- Keep it warm but professional
- Use "I'm excited" / "I'm drawn to" / "I'd be proud to"

### ❌ Don't:
- Use corporate buzzword soup ("synergize", "leverage", etc.)
- Sound desperate or overeager
- Repeat CV content verbatim
- Use overly formal language ("I am writing to formally apply...")
- Make claims without evidence

### Skye's natural voice markers:
- "I thrive in..."
- "I bring..."
- "I'd be glad to..."
- "Warm regards"
- Calm, capable, collaborative tone
- Solutions-first mindset references

## PDF Generation Rules

When creating application packages:

1. **Cover Letter:** Generate as PDF with:
   - Header: Skye Harvey, contact details
   - Date: Current date
   - Recipient: "Dear [Hiring Manager/Team Name]" (use specific name if provided in job ad)
   - Body: Framework above
   - Footer: "Warm regards, Skye Harvey"

2. **CV:** Use existing PDF (cv-digital-pm-wapha.pdf or cv-senior-event-manager.pdf)

3. **Selection Criteria:** If needed, generate as separate PDF with:
   - Header: "Selection Criteria Response — Skye Harvey"
   - Each criterion as heading
   - Response below
   - Page numbers if >1 page

4. **File naming convention:**
   - `SkyeHarvey-CoverLetter-[COMPANY]-[ROLE]-[DATE].pdf`
   - `SkyeHarvey-CV-[VARIANT]-[DATE].pdf`
   - `SkyeHarvey-SelectionCriteria-[COMPANY]-[DATE].pdf`

## Quality Check Before Flagging as "Standout"

A job should only get a full application package if:

✅ Salary meets minimum ($115K+) OR is not disclosed but company/role suggests appropriate level  
✅ Explicitly remote/WFH OR located in WA  
✅ Timezone is WA-compatible (no "must be available 9-5 AEST" unless flexible)  
✅ Role closely matches either Digital PM or Event Manager experience  
✅ Company has strong online presence (not a red-flag/scam posting)  
✅ Job description is well-written and detailed (not vague/generic)  
✅ Application method is clear (email, portal, etc.)

If 5+ of these are true → **Generate full package**  
If 3-4 of these are true → **Flag as "strong match" for Skye to review**  
If <3 → **List in daily email but don't generate package**
