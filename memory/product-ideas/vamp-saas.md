# Product Idea: VAMPaaS - Marine Asset Management Platform

## Pitch
VAMP (Vessel Asset Management Platform) rebuilt to replace Rise-X SaaS. Could be offered as a standalone product to marine companies, ports, and shipping operators.

## What We Have
- Full-stack marine asset management (vessels, work orders, inspections, media, audit)
- Multi-party collaboration with role-based permissions
- Hash-chained audit trail (blockchain-equivalent)
- Ready for Render deployment

## What Missing
- BFMP (Biofouling Management Plan) PDF templates
- External stakeholder portal
- Multi-org workflow automation

## Revenue Model
- **SaaS subscription**: $99-499/month (tiered by vessel count)
- **Setup fee**: $2K-5K for on-premise/custom deployments
- **Training/support**: $500/hour

## Target Market
- Australian ports (Fremantle pilot = proof point)
- Regional shipping operators
- ROV/diving companies
- Naval fleet managers

## Effort to Complete
- 2-3 weeks for BFMP templates
- 2-4 weeks for external portal
- Deployment + marketing: 1 week

## Next Steps
1. Deploy VAMP to Render (blocking)
2. Test with MarineStream real data
3. Add BFMP template
4. Create marketing one-pager
