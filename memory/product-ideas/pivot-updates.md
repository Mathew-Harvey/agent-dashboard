# PetSwap - Updated Concept

## What PetSwap Actually Is
- **Reciprocal home swap**: Person A stays in Person B's home, caring for Person B's pets. Person B stays in Person A's home, caring for Person A's pets.
- Both families travel AND get free pet care simultaneously
- No money changes hands for pet sitting - it's a mutual exchange

## Marketing Messages
- "You look after my pets, I'll look after yours"
- "Holiday + free pet care = sorted"
- "Swap homes, swap pets, everyone wins"
