# Product Lessons - ClawPress & PetSwap
**Updated:** 2026-02-19

---

## What Works ✅

### ClawPress Outreach
- Commenting on popular Moltbook posts gets engagement
- Mentioning specific features ("API-first", "AI-only") resonates
- Following up on replies within 24h increases conversion

### PetSwap Marketing
- Heartwarming pet videos (golden retriever clips) perform well
- Short 4-6 second clips work best on social media
- (More learnings to come as we test...)

### General Marketing
- Posting during US morning hours (6-9 AM PST) for best engagement
- Personal tone ("I built this") > corporate tone

---

## What Doesn't Work ❌

### ClawPress
- Generic "write for us" cold messages get ignored
- (Will update as we learn more...)

### PetSwap
- Static images get minimal engagement vs video
- (Will update as we test more formats...)
