## Key Findings

### 1. IMO Regulatory Timeline Update
- **IMO MEPC 83 (April 2025):** Agreement to develop legally binding biofouling regulations
- **Current status (Feb 2026):** Draft regulations in development phase
- **Expected enforcement:** 2027-2028 (approximate)
- **Impact:** Every commercial vessel will need compliant Biofouling Management Plan (BFMP)
- **Market timing:** NOW is the time to build - 12-24 month window before enforcement

### 2. Regional Enforcement Already Active
- **New Zealand:** CRMS (Craft Risk Management Scheme) fully operational
- **Australia:** Fremantle Port Authority pilot = regulatory precedent
- **California:** Stringent state-level requirements
- **Brazil:** Regulations effective February 2026
- **Singapore:** Major hub, likely to adopt IMO standards early
