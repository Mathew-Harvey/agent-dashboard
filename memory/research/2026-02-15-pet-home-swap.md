# Research: Pet Home Swap (2026-02-15)

## Competitor Analysis

### HomeExchange (World Leader)
- **Members:** 200,000+ in 155 countries
- **Model:** $235/year subscription (unlimited exchanges)
- **Innovation:** GuestPoints system for non-reciprocal exchanges
- **Features:** Property damage coverage, member support, Facebook groups

### Love Home Swap
- UK-based, similar model
- Focus on quality listings

### TrustedHousesitters
- Different model: you sit others' pets for free
- Get free accommodation in return
- 100k+ members

## Market Insights
- Home exchange market is proven but dominated by generalist platforms
- Pet owner niche is underserved
- Trust/verification is key barrier
- Network effect: hardest part is getting liquidity

## Opportunity: Pet-Specific Angle
- Pet profiles (breed, temperament, care needs)
- Vet verification
- Pet-friendly amenities filters
- "Pet parents" community vibe
- Avoid boarding costs (key pain point: $30-60/night)

## Validation Strategy
1. Start in Australia (smaller, tighter community)
2. Target specific dog communities (breed groups, parks)
3. Free to start, premium for verification
4. Partner with vets, pet shops for distribution

## MVP Features
1. Listing (photos, details, pet info)
2. Search/filter (location, pet type, amenities)
3. Messaging between members
4. Simple verification (email, ID)

## Revenue Estimate
- 100 users @ $9.99/mo = ~$1,000/mo
- 500 users @ $9.99/mo = ~$5,000/mo
- Swap fees + premium listings = additional
