# Automation Products People Actually Pay For
**Date:** 2026-02-19  
**Topic:** Thursday rotation - Automation products people pay for  
**Research Session:** Daytime deep dive

## Executive Summary
Automation is a mature, competitive market with clear winners and price points. People pay $3-$20/mo for personal productivity automation, $50-$200/mo for team automation, and $200-$1000+/mo for enterprise. **Key insight:** The market has shifted from "workflow automation" (Zapier) to "AI-powered automation with human oversight" (Relay.app) and "vertical-specific automation" (Magical for healthcare, Apify for web scraping).

## Market Segments & Pricing

### 1. General Workflow Automation (Saturated)
**Zapier** - Market leader, established since 2011
- Free: 100 tasks/mo, 2-step Zaps
- Professional: $19.99/mo (billed annually) - multi-step, webhooks, premium apps
- Team: $69/mo - 25 users, shared workspace
- Enterprise: Custom pricing - unlimited users, SSO, TAM

**Key insight:** Zapier's moat is 8,000+ integrations and network effects. Hard to compete head-on.

**Relay.app** - Rising star with "human-in-the-loop" differentiation
- Unknown pricing (couldn't extract from page)
- User testimonials are GLOWING: "wayyyyy better than Zapier"
- Unique value prop: pause workflows for human decisions/judgment
- Much better UI/UX than Zapier (multiple testimonials confirm)
- Focus on team collaboration, not just automation

**Opportunity:** Relay.app proves there's room for innovation even in saturated markets if you solve a REAL pain point (automated workflows breaking when edge cases occur).

**n8n** - Open source alternative
- Unlimited workflows and users
- Pricing based on monthly executions (didn't get exact numbers)
- Self-hosted option available
- Developer-friendly

### 2. Web Scraping & Data Extraction (High-Value Niche)
**Apify** - Web scraping platform with detailed usage-based pricing
- Free: $5 prepaid usage
- Starter: $29/mo + pay-as-you-go
- Scale: $199/mo + PAYG ($0.25/CU vs $0.3)
- Business: $999/mo + PAYG ($0.2/CU)
- Enterprise: Custom

**Pricing breakdown:**
- Compute units: $0.20-$0.30 per GB RAM-hour
- Residential proxies: $7-$8/GB
- Datacenter proxies: $0.60-$1 per IP
- SERP proxies: $1.70-$2.50 per 1000 SERPs

**Key customers:** 
- "2x leads to drive business"
- "28M+ AI chats resolved for Intercom Fin"
- "800+ retailers monitored across EU for compliance"

**Insight:** Web scraping is a high-margin business because customers NEED the data for revenue-critical operations (lead gen, compliance monitoring, competitive intelligence).

### 3. Personal Productivity Tools (Proven Willingness to Pay)
**TextExpander** - Text snippet automation
- Individual: $3.33/mo ($39.96/year)
- Business: $8.33/mo ($99.96/year) - team sharing, admin controls
- Growth: $10.83/mo ($129.96/year) - advanced analytics
- Enterprise: Custom pricing - SSO, SCIM, custom MSA

**Keyboard Maestro** (Mac only)
- One-time purchase: $36 USD
- Upgrade pricing: $25 for existing users
- No subscription model

**Alfred Powerpack** (Mac only)
- Pricing not extracted but similar to Keyboard Maestro
- Lifetime license model
- Mac productivity automation (workflows, snippets, file search)

**Insight:** People will pay $3-10/mo for tools that save them hours every week. Mac users especially willing to pay for quality productivity tools.

### 4. Vertical-Specific Automation (Highest Margins)
**Magical** - Pivoted to healthcare AI employees
- Now focused exclusively on healthcare vertical
- "Agentic AI employees" for clinical, administrative, financial workflows
- Enterprise pricing (no public pricing)

**Insight:** Pivoting from horizontal (browser automation for everyone) to vertical (healthcare only) suggests that's where the money is. Healthcare has budget and regulatory needs that justify premium pricing.

## What People Actually Pay For

### Proven Categories (High Confidence)
1. **Time savings automation** - If it saves 5+ hours/week, people pay $10-50/mo
2. **Revenue-enabling automation** - Lead gen, scraping, monitoring - people pay $100-1000/mo
3. **Compliance/regulatory** - Healthcare, finance, legal - unlimited budgets if it prevents fines
4. **Team collaboration tools** - 5-25 person teams pay $50-200/mo for shared automation
5. **Text expansion/snippets** - Individual power users pay $3-10/mo for years

### Price Sensitivity Observations
- **Free tier is table stakes** - Every successful tool has a generous free tier
- **$20/mo is the magic number** for individual professionals (Zapier Pro, TextExpander Business)
- **$100-200/mo** is the team plan sweet spot (5-10 users)
- **$500+/mo** requires enterprise features (SSO, compliance, dedicated support)

### What Doesn't Work
- Pure horizontal plays in saturated markets (competing with Zapier on features alone)
- One-time purchases for cloud services (Keyboard Maestro only works because it's desktop software)
- Complex pricing models (Apify's 47 different price points might hurt conversions)

## Gaps & Opportunities

### Gap 1: AI-Powered Vertical Automation for SMBs
**The Problem:** Relay.app and Magical prove AI + automation works, but they target different segments (Relay = horizontal teams, Magical = healthcare enterprise). There's a gap for **AI-powered vertical automation for SMBs in specific industries**.

**Potential verticals:**
- **Marine/maritime** (Mat's domain!) - Compliance reporting, vessel scheduling, biofouling tracking
- **Construction** - Permit tracking, subcontractor management, safety compliance
- **Legal** - Document automation, deadline tracking, client intake
- **Accounting/bookkeeping** - Receipt processing, expense categorization, tax prep

**Why it works:**
- SMBs have budget ($100-500/mo range) but not dev resources
- Vertical-specific = you can charge 3-5x more than horizontal tools
- AI handles edge cases that break traditional automation
- Network effects within industry (one construction company tells another)

**Confidence:** 7/10 - Proven model (Magical pivoted this way) but requires deep domain knowledge

### Gap 2: "Automation-as-a-Service" for Non-Technical Users
**The Problem:** Tools like n8n and Zapier require users to BUILD their automations. Many people want the OUTCOME, not to learn another tool.

**Solution:** Done-for-you automation service
- Customer describes what they want automated (via chat, form, or call)
- We build the automation using Zapier/n8n/Make (whatever works)
- We charge monthly for maintenance and monitoring
- Upsell: Additional automations as they think of them

**Pricing model:**
- Setup fee: $200-500 per automation
- Monthly: $50-200/mo per automation maintained
- Target: 20 customers = $2,000-8,000/mo recurring

**Why it works:**
- People pay MORE for done-for-you vs DIY (TextExpander $3 DIY vs $500 setup + $200/mo DFY)
- Automation consulting is a real business (many freelancers do this)
- Can start immediately with no code/product development

**Confidence:** 8/10 - Proven business model, can start tomorrow, fits Mat's consulting background

### Gap 3: Micro-SaaS for Specific Automation Needs
**The Problem:** Zapier charges per task. For high-volume, simple automations, this gets expensive fast.

**Examples of successful micro-SaaS:**
- **Email warmup tools** - $20-50/mo to send scheduled emails gradually
- **Instagram auto-DM** - $30-100/mo to auto-respond to Story mentions
- **LinkedIn automation** - $50-150/mo for connection requests and follow-ups
- **Price monitoring** - $20-50/mo to track competitor prices and alert

**Why it works:**
- Unlimited usage model = customers prefer it to pay-per-task
- Simple, focused value prop = easy to market
- Can build MVP in 1-2 weeks
- Charge $30-100/mo = 100 customers = $3,000-10,000/mo

**Confidence:** 6/10 - Competitive, requires product development, marketing skill

### Gap 4: OpenClaw Skills Marketplace (ClawHub)
**The Problem:** OpenClaw users need automations, but building skills from scratch is hard. ClawHub exists but doesn't have a thriving paid skill ecosystem yet.

**Opportunity:**
- Build high-quality, revenue-generating OpenClaw skills
- Sell on ClawHub marketplace
- Price: $10-50 per skill or $5-20/mo for subscription skills
- Create "skill bundles" for specific use cases

**Example skills to build:**
- **Marine compliance automation** - Auto-generate biofouling reports, track vessel schedules
- **Freelancer productivity suite** - Time tracking, invoice generation, client follow-ups
- **Content creator tools** - Video transcription, social media cross-posting, engagement tracking
- **E-commerce automation** - Order processing, inventory alerts, supplier communication

**Why it works:**
- OpenClaw is new = first-mover advantage
- Mat has insider knowledge of the platform
- Can leverage existing skills as templates
- Build once, sell forever (passive income potential)

**Confidence:** 7/10 - Platform is early but growing, requires quality skills to stand out
