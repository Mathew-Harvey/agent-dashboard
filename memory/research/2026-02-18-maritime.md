# Maritime/Marine Tech Opportunities Research
**Date:** 2026-02-18 | **Topic:** Wednesday - Maritime/Marine Tech Focus

## Executive Summary
Marine tech is heating up with ROVs, AUVs, USVs, and underwater robotics. Key areas: hull cleaning robots, biofouling management software, and maritime SaaS. Australia's Fremantle Port Authority just approved first commercial in-water hull cleaning - a regulatory breakthrough with national implications.

## Key Findings

### 1. Underwater Hull Cleaning Robotics (HIGH OPPORTUNITY)
- **Market:** Growing demand for ROV-based hull cleaning to replace diver operations
- **Players:** Hydrex (Belgium), VideoRay, Kongsberg, Teledyne
- **Gap:** Fully autonomous hull cleaning robots are still rare
- **Australian context:** Franmarine just secured Australia's first commercial in-water hull cleaning agreement with Fremantle Port Authority - regulatory precedent being watched nationally

### 2. Maritime Software & APIs
- **Veson Nautical:** Major player in maritime freight software
- **Shipfix:** Communication/productivity tool with WhatsApp integration
- **Gap:** Specialized APIs for biofouling management, compliance tracking, hull inspection scheduling
- **Opportunity:** Niche SaaS for navy/commercial fleet biofouling tracking

### 3. Ocean Robotics Events
- **Oceanology International 2026** (March 10-12, London) - 8,000 attendees, 500 exhibitors
- **OCEANS 2026** (May 25-28, Sanya, China)
- Major focus: USVs, AUVs, underwater inspection, CUI protection

### 4. Defence/Military Maritime
- **REMUS 100** AUV achieving 935 missions with near-perfect availability (Australian Maritime College + HII)
- NATO focus on undersea infrastructure protection
- **Opportunity:** Dual-use tech (defence + commercial)

### 5. Australian Maritime Context
- **Fremantle Port Authority pilot:** First commercial in-water cleaning in Australia
- **RAN sustainment:** Babcock Australasia prime contractor, looking for biofouling solutions
- **Svitzer Australia:** Major tug fleet proposal in progress
- **US DoD NextMRO Prize Challenge:** Potential $50M+ contracts
