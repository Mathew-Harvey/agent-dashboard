# Marketing Research Log - Feb 25, 2026

## Muscle-up Reddit Research
**Hot topics/questions found:**
- "To people that can do muscle-ups, how did you progress towards doing one?" - 21 upvotes, 34 comments
  - Key insights: People want specific progression paths, struggle with transition
- "I finally achieved the muscle up at age 38" - 1792 upvotes
  - Top tips: False grip training, weighted pull-ups, band assistance
- "From 2 Pull-ups to 12 Muscle-ups in 1 year" - 2598 upvotes
  - Success story: 26M, 95kg, 1.89m (tall/heavy lifter)
- "How many of you can do a muscle up?" - 68 upvotes
  - Key point: explosive power + technique + coordination needed

## Handstand Reddit Research
**Hot topics/questions found:**
- "How I Learned to Handstand in 1 Month" - 942 upvotes
  - 0 to 20 seconds in 1 month - achievable goals
- "My Handstand Journey: What I wish I knew when I began" - 1965 upvotes
  - Balance is biggest challenge, takes time
- "Handstand is HARD, anyone saying otherwise is a liar" - 302 upvotes
  - Real talk: takes YEARS, not months
- "I'm losing all hope with handstands" - recent post (Feb 15, 2026)
  - Common issues: hip alignment, kicking up consistently
