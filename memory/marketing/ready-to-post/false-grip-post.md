# False Grip Protocol Post

**Subreddit:** r/calisthenics

**Title:** The false grip protocol I used after 12 months of elbow tendonitis (what actually worked)

---

**Body:**

False grip is where most people stall. Here's what actually worked for me after destroying my elbows for a year.

**The mistake everyone makes:**
Going straight to false grip on the rings before your skin and tendons are ready. You try to hold on, slip down, and repeat. Your elbows hate you.

**What actually works:**

1. **Start with thick-grip training** - use a towel, thick rope, or gymnastics ring straps for 2-3 months before touching bare rings. Build up to 30-second holds.

2. **Wrist conditioning comes first** - your wrist needs to handle the load. Wrist curls, extensions, and just hanging in false grip position (not pulling, just holding) for time.

3. **The 30-day false grip challenge:**
   - Week 1-2: 3 x 10-second holds (support position)
   - Week 3-4: 3 x 20-second holds + negative pulls

4. **Skin calluses, not rips** - use a pumice stone regularly. Thin, hard calluses hold better than thick torn ones.

**The truth:** It took me 4 months of dedicated false grip work before I could hold it long enough to complete a muscle-up. Rush this and you'll set yourself back months with tendonitis.

Happy to answer questions if you're stuck on this step.
