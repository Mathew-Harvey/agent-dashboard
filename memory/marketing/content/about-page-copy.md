#### About The Bodyweight Gym

I left a career in marine science to open a calisthenics studio in Perth. That was 2017. The Bodyweight Gym ran until 2021 — four years of coaching everyone from complete beginners to competitive athletes.

In Perth's fitness scene, two skills made you immediately hireable as a trainer: a strict ring muscle-up and a freestanding handstand. So that's what I learned to teach.

Problem was, I had no gymnastics background. I was a 30-something adult learning from scratch. The first 12 months nearly broke me. Elbow tendonitis that wouldn't go away because I kept progressing too fast. Muscles ready, tendons weren't.

It took two years of trial and error, countless failed attempts, and one breakthrough moment after a night out with mates when someone asked me to show them something on the rings I'd installed in my apartment. Liquid courage or audience pressure — I nailed my first strict ring muscle-up that night.

Since then, I've coached hundreds of people through their first muscle-up and handstand. I learned everything the hard way so you don't have to.

This programme contains everything I know. Six levels. Graduation tests at each stage. No fluff. No shortcuts.

— Mat  
The Bodyweight Gym, Perth WA
