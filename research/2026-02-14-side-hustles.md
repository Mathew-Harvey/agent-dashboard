# Research: Side Hustles & Money-Making Ideas

Date: 2026-02-14

## Key Takeaways

- **Side jobs** (side hustles) are extra jobs taken in addition to primary employment to supplement income
- Nearly 39% of Americans reported having a side job in 2023
- Common side hustles include: copywriting, e-commerce, affiliate marketing, social media marketing, freelance web design, tutoring, graphic design, freelance writing
- Gen Z has surpassed Millennials as the generation with the highest rate of working side jobs
- Most common reason: additional disposable income, paying off student loans, financial "safety net"
